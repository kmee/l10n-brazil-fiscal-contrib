NFCom

Issuance of the Electronic Communication Services Invoice (NFCom, model 62) from the Odoo fiscal document (l10n_br_fiscal.document), following the same pattern as l10n_br_nfe and l10n_br_mdfe: the l10n_br_nfcom_spec models are stacked onto the document and the line (spec_driven_model) and the XML is built with the nfelib bindings.

The NFCom replaces models 21 (NFSC) and 22 (NFST) for communication and telecommunication services (Ajuste SINIEF 7/22) and has been mandatory since 2025-11-01 (Ajuste SINIEF 34/24). The IBSCBS group of the tax reform follows NT 2025.001 RTC.

The module:

  • creates fiscal document type 62, which l10n_br_fiscal does not have yet;
  • generates the access key in the NFCom format (MOC NFCom 1.00a, item 2.1.3: cUF, AAMM, CNPJ, mod, serie, nNF, tpEmis, nSiteAutoriz, 7-digit cNF and cDV in modulo 11, item 7.3);
  • builds the groups ide, emit, dest, assinante, det (prod and imposto), total and infAdic, and the infNFComSupl with the QR Code URL of the state authorizer (services page of the NFCom Portal);
  • carries ICMS (groups ICMS00, ICMS20, ICMS40, ICMS51, ICMS90 and ICMSSN, by CST and company regime), PIS, COFINS and IBS and CBS (IBSCBS on the item and IBSCBSTot on the total) computed by the l10n_br_fiscal fiscal engine;
  • computes the item vProd and vNF by rules G86 and G137 of Annex I of the MOC;
  • signs the XML with the company certificate and validates it against the nfelib XSD when the document is confirmed, storing the signed XML in the authorization event;
  • builds and signs the cancellation event (110111, MOC item 6.1), validated against the XSD.

Transmission to the authorizer is not in the module yet (see the roadmap): on sending, the document warns that transmission is not available.

Usage

  1. On the company, set the OCA electronic document processor, the digital certificate and the NFCom environment (electronic documents tab).
  2. Create a series for document type 62 on the company.
  3. On the product, enter the NFCom item classification (cClass, table of MOC item 7.8) and the unit of measure (uMed).
  4. Create the fiscal document with type 62, fill in the subscriber data in the NFCom tab and confirm: the XML is generated, signed and validated, and schema errors appear on the document.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • Transmission (goal: a contribution to nfelib). nfelib 3.3.0 has the NFCom bindings, but not the SOAP bindings (nfelib/nfcom/soap/v1_0) nor the client (nfelib/nfcom/client/v1_0) on top of brazil_fiscal_client, as already exist for NF-e, CT-e and MDF-e. The WSDL of the services NFComRecepcao, NFComConsulta, NFComRecepcaoEvento and NFComStatusServico are only served with mutual authentication (ICP-Brasil certificate) and MOC NFCom 1.00a (item 3.4.1) writes the namespace with a different case from the one used in the schemas (NFCom x nfcom), so the name of the nfcomDadosMsg element and the namespace of each service must be checked in the WSDL before generating the bindings. Reception is synchronous and the message is compressed with GZip and encoded in Base64 (MOC, items 3.4.1 and 4.2); the authorizers are MG, MS, MT and SVRS (services page of the NFCom Portal). With the client in nfelib: submission, query, protocol (nfcomProc) and sending of the cancellation event already built by this module.
  • NFCom schema_validation in nfelib: the CommonMixin does not know the NFCom XSD path and the module passes the path explicitly.
  • Layout: the schemas are those of the package PL_NFCOM_1.00_NT2026.002 RTC_1.01 shipped with nfelib 3.3.0. NT 2026.004 v1.01 (production on 2026-12-14) and NT 2026.005 v1.00 (Simples Nacional) are not in nfelib yet: regenerate l10n_br_nfcom_spec when they are.
  • NT 2026.002 rules that depend on registration data: CNPJCobrTerc for items with cClass of group 110 (third-party billing) and rejection 310 (IBS/CBS group required for CRT 3).
  • IBSCBS group: only gIBSCBS; missing are gEstornoCred, deferral, tax refund, reduction for government procurement and the municipal IBS separate from the state one (exported as zero, as in NF-e).
  • Billing: groups gFat (competence period, due date, barcode, automatic debit, PIX), gFatCentral, gCofat, gSub (replacement), gFidelidade, pgtoVinc and gRespTec, and recurring billing from contracts.
  • Items: gProcRef, gRessarc, ICMSUFDest, FUST, FUNTTEL, withholdings per item (retTrib), indSemCST and validation of the CFOP against the list of MOC item 7.7 and of cClass against the table of item 7.8.
  • Contingency (tpEmis 2, dhCont, xJust and QR Code signature).
  • DANFE-COM (Annex II of the MOC).
  • Document type 62: move the record to the l10n_br_fiscal data and include 62 in the IBS and CBS tax classifications (tax_classification) that apply to the NFCom.
  • NFCom XML import.
