NFS-e Nacional Construction Work (obra)

This module reports the construction work group (serv/obra, TCInfoObra) of the NFS-e Nacional DPS, which l10n_br_nfse_nacional does not fill.

The construction site is an address (res.partner) that carries the work registration (CNO, or the former CEI), the Brazilian Real Estate Registry code (CIB) and the municipal real estate registration. The fiscal document takes the site from its shipping address and copies these codes; all of them can be changed on each document.

The group identifies the work by its CNO (cObra), or by its CIB (cCIB) when there is no CNO, or by the site address (end) when there is neither. The municipal registration (inscImobFisc) goes along when informed.

Rule E0370 (Anexo VI of the NFS-e Nacional, leiaute 1.04.01 published with NT 009) makes the group mandatory for the national taxation codes 07.02.01, 07.02.02, 07.04.01, 07.05.01, 07.05.02, 07.06.01, 07.06.02, 07.07.01, 07.08.01, 07.17.01, 07.19.01, 14.14.03 and 14.14.04: the module refuses to build the DPS of these services without the work identification. For the other services the group is reported only when the document has a CNO or a CIB.

Usage

Register the construction site as an address of the customer and fill the CNO/CEI, CIB and municipal real estate registration in the Fiscal tab of the address.

On the NFS-e, choose the site as shipping address. The Construction Work tab of the fiscal document shows the site and its codes, which can be changed for that document. The DPS XML carries them in the serv/obra group.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • Rule E0373 (the CIB is 7 characters and a check digit) is checked by the ADN only: the module checks the length of the schema, not the check digit, whose algorithm is not in the leiaute.
  • Rule E0380 (the CEP of the site belongs to the municipality of the service location) is not checked: the module has no CEP to municipality table.
  • Rule E0384 asks for the foreign address group (endExt) when the service location is abroad (cPaisPrestacao). The module chooses between CEP and endExt by the country of the site address.
  • The fields are on the fiscal document form only; the invoice form of l10n_br_account does not show them.
