NFS-e Nacional Tax Base Adjustment (deducao)

This module reports the tax base adjustment group (valores/vAjusteBC, TCInfoAjusteBC) of the NFS-e Nacional DPS, which l10n_br_nfse_nacional does not fill: the deduction from the ISSQN base and the documents that support the deductions and the refunds (documentos/docAjusteBC).

The ISSQN deduction comes from the ISSQN Deduction Value of the fiscal line (vAjusteBCISSQN), the same field the NF-e reports as vDeducao.

The documents are listed on the NFS-e, each with the adjustment type (tpAjusteBC), the document amount and the part of it applied to this NFS-e. A document can be an electronic document of the national repository (NFS-e, NF-e, CT-e or other, by its access key), a fiscal document outside the repository or a non fiscal document. Choosing a fiscal document registered in Odoo fills the key, the amount, the dates and the supplier, all of which can be changed.

The module checks the rules of the group that do not depend on the municipal parameters or on the national registries (Anexo VI of the NFS-e Nacional, leiaute 1.04.01 published with NT 009):

  • on each document: E0464 and E0465 (description only and always for the types 99 and 199), E0469 (applied amount up to the document amount), E0472 and E0474 (competence not after the issue date), E0470 (description of an electronic document of type Other), E0460 (access key of 50 digits for an NFS-e and 44 for an NF-e or CT-e), E0476 (fiscal document outside the repository only with a competence before 2026) and E0479 (types 101, 103, 104 and 199 need a fiscal document);
  • when building the DPS: E0435 (a service not taxed by the ISSQN only takes the refunds, types 101 to 199), E0462 (the ISSQN deduction excludes the documents of types 2 to 99), E0463 (services 06.01 and 06.02 only take the types 9 and 199) and E0467 (applied amount up to the service amount).

Usage

Inform the ISSQN deduction on the fiscal line, as for any other document, or list the supporting documents in the Tax Base Adjustment tab of the NFS-e. For a document already in Odoo (a supplier NF-e of materials, a subcontractor NFS-e), choose it in Fiscal Document and inform the applied amount. The DPS XML carries them in the valores/vAjusteBC group.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • The percentage of ISSQN base adjustment (pAjusteBCISSQN, rule E0453) is not reported: the fiscal line has a deduction amount only.
  • Rules that depend on the municipal parameters or on national registries are left to the ADN: E0436 (MEI), E0445 (special tax regime), E0448 (municipal exemption benefit), E0437 (IBS/CBS classification without taxation), E0449 to E0452 and E0455 (agreement status of the municipality and Simples Nacional services), E0444, E0446, E0447 and E0454 (municipal parameters of the service), E0466 (IBGE code), E0478 to E0488 (supplier registration) and E1538 (NIF of a foreign supplier).
  • Only the first line of the NFS-e is reported, as in l10n_br_nfse_nacional, so the ISSQN deduction is the one of that line.
  • The fields are on the fiscal document form only; the invoice form of l10n_br_account does not show them.
