This module fills the transport subgroups of the NF-e (group X,
``transp``) that the localization leaves empty: the vehicle
(``veicTransp``), the trailers (``reboque``) and the ICMS retention of
the transport (``retTransp``). The freight modality (``modFrete``), the
carrier (``transporta``) and the volumes (``vol``) stay with
``l10n_br_delivery_nfe``.

Vehicle and trailers come from the carrier vehicles of
``l10n_br_delivery`` (plate, state and ANTT code), that until now never
reached the NF-e. On the fiscal document (*Delivery* tab):

- vehicle: by default the vehicle of the carrier, when it has only one;
  it can be changed;
- trailers: up to 5 vehicles (layout limit).

Each one becomes a ``veicTransp`` or ``reboque`` group with the plate
(upper case, without separators), the state and the ANTT code (RNTC, up
to 20 characters). They are left out of the XML in interstate operations
(rule X02-20, rejection 868, NT 2017.002), in the NFC-e and in the NF-e
with the simplified DANFE (rules X18-10 and X22-10, rejections 756 and
757).

The ICMS retention of the transport is used when the issuer retains the
ICMS of a freight service (for instance one hired from an independent
carrier): service value, retention base (by default the service value),
rate, ICMS retained (base times rate), freight CFOP and city of the
taxable event (by default the city of the company). It is refused in the
NFC-e and in the NF-e with the simplified DANFE (rule X11-10, rejection
755).

Before the NF-e is confirmed the module refuses a plate that does not
match the layout pattern and an ICMS retention without CFOP or city.

Configuration
=============

- Register the vehicles of each carrier (*Delivery Methods*, vehicles)
  with plate, state and ANTT code, and keep them active.

Usage
=====

1. On the fiscal document of the NF-e, *Delivery* tab, choose the
   carrier: its vehicle is filled when it has only one.
2. Choose the vehicle and the trailers, and fill the ICMS retention of
   the transport when the issuer retains it.
3. Confirm the NF-e: the XML carries the ``veicTransp``, ``reboque`` and
   ``retTransp`` groups filled.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The vehicle is not carried from the sale order or the delivery to the
  invoice: it is chosen on the fiscal document (or computed from the
  carrier).
- The optional extension of rule X02-20 to internal operations between
  different cities (at the discretion of each state) is not applied.
- The choice between vehicle/trailers, wagon (``vagao``) and ferry
  (``balsa``) is not handled: wagon and ferry are not filled.
- The freight CFOP of the ICMS retention is not checked against the CFOP
  table of the NF-e portal (rule X16-10, rejection 722).
- The ICMS retention is typed in: it is not computed from the freight
  service purchase.
