This module reports the real estate group (``IBSCBS/imovel``,
TCRTCInfoImovel) that the tax reform added to the NFS-e Nacional DPS and
that ``l10n_br_nfse_nacional`` does not fill:

- ``cMun``: city of the property, taken from the address of the first
  unit or, lacking it, from the ISSQN city of the service line;
- ``gLocacao`` (TCRTCLocacaoImovel): the lease data of subitem 99.03,
  with the co-ownership share, the lease total amount and discounts
  (taken from the service line and editable) and the original due date;
- ``gUnidImob`` (TCRTCUnidadesImobiliarias): the real estate units, each
  identified by its CIB or by its address, with the municipal real
  estate registration and the amounts included in the rent that are not
  part of the IBS/CBS base (``gAjusteBCLocImoveis``,
  TCRTCValoresBCImoveis).

The group is part of the IBSCBS group, so it is reported only when the
NFS-e carries the tax reform data of ``l10n_br_nfse_nacional``.

The module follows the rules of the group in Anexo VI of the NFS-e
Nacional (leiaute 1.04.01 published with NT 009): the group is reported
for the real estate operation indicators (020101, 020201, 020202, 020301
and 020401) outside the construction services, as rules E0928, E0931 and
E0932 require; the lease data goes along for subitem 99.03 only (E0936
and E0937). It refuses to build the DPS without units for the indicators
020101, 020201 and 020301 (E0938), with unit adjustments outside subitem
99.03 (E0939), with the social reducer outside the residential lease NBS
1.1002.10.00 (E0944) or with an adjustment above the lease total amount
(E0966). The co-ownership share goes from 0 to 100% (E0940) and only the
adjustment type 99 takes a description (E0949 and E0954).

Usage
=====

On the NFS-e of a real estate operation, choose on the service line the
real estate operation indicator and the tax reform classification, then
fill the *Real Estate* tab of the fiscal document: the units (CIB or
address) and, for a lease, the co-ownership share, the amounts and the
items included in the rent. The DPS XML carries them in the
``IBSCBS/imovel`` group.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The lease total amount (``vTotOper``) starts from the service amount
  of the line, which is the lessor share when the property has several
  owners: change it to the total of the lease in that case, as the
  leiaute asks.
- Rules E0929 (IBGE code of the city) and E0933 (CIB check digit, whose
  algorithm is not in the leiaute) are left to the ADN.
- The condominium subitem 99.05 (rule without code that makes the group
  mandatory) and the debit and credit adjustment NFS-e
  (``tpNFSeDebito``, ``tpNFSeCredito``) are not handled: they are not in
  the schema of the DPS 1.01.
- The operation type with real estate (``tpOper``) and the movable goods
  group (``bensMoveis``) of the IBSCBS group are not reported.
- The fields are on the fiscal document form only; the invoice form of
  ``l10n_br_account`` does not show them.
