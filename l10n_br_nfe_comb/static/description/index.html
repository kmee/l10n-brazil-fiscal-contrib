NF-e Fuels (comb)

This module reports the fuel group of the NF-e items (group LA, comb) and checks it against the SEFAZ validation rules.

The ANP product code (cProdANP) and description (descANP), the LPG percentages and starting value (pGLP, pGNn, pGNi, vPart), the CODIF, the biodiesel percentage (pBio) and the fuel origin (origComb) are set once on the product and the fiscal document lines of that product point to them. The group can be replaced on each line, for instance to add the CIDE, the pump meter (encerrante) or the quantity at ambient temperature (qTemp).

The consumption state (UFCons) is the state of the fuel group when it is set, otherwise the state of the delivery address of the document (EX for a foreign customer).

The LPG percentages are written with two decimals, so that their sum is exactly 100. The ANP code is checked against the layout (9 digits) and each origin percentage must be greater than 0.

Before the NF-e is confirmed the module refuses the data the SEFAZ would reject:

  • rule LA03c-10 (rejection 461, NT 2016.002): LPG percentages for an ANP code other than 210203001;
  • rule LA03c-20 (rejection 855, NT 2016.002): LPG percentages that do not add up to 100;
  • rule LA11-20 (rejection 379, NT 2015.002): pump meter in an NF-e (model 55) with a CFOP other than 5656 and 5667;
  • rule LA16-10 (rejection 380, NT 2015.002): pump meter at the end not greater than at the start;
  • rule LA18-20 (rejection 909, NT 2023.001): LPG with natural gas and no fuel origin, except for final consumers, complementary and return NF-e and the CFOP 5922 and 6922;
  • rules LA21-10 and LA21-20 (rejection 958, NT 2023.001): origin percentages that do not add up to 100 (separately for national and imported origin in the LPG codes 210203001, 210203003, 210203004 and 210203005), with a tolerance of 0.1.

Configuration

On the product form, Fiscal tab, fill the Fuel (ANP) field with the ANP product code and description of the SIMP table and, when they apply, the LPG percentages, the biodiesel percentage and the fuel origin.

Usage

Invoice a fuel product as usual. The fiscal document line shows the fuel group in the Fuel tab, where it can be replaced by one with the CIDE or the pump meter of the sale; the NF-e XML carries the comb tag of the item.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • The rules that depend on official tables are not enforced: LA02-10 (rejection 761, ANP code missing from the SIMP table), LA17-10 and LA17-20 (rejections 907 and 908, biodiesel percentage and the table of fuels subject to the single phase taxation), LA18-10 and LA18-30 (rejections 909 and 747, fuel origin and the same table) and LA01-20 (rejection 660, CFOP of fuel without the fuel group).
  • Rule LA01-30 (rejection 1106, fuel cClassTrib of the tax reform without the fuel group) and rule I13-20 (rejection 854, LPG with a taxable unit other than kg) are not enforced.
  • The CIDE is not computed: its group is typed on the line.
  • The biodiesel and origin percentages are stored without decimals.
  • The fuel group is shared by the lines that use it: changing the product group changes it for the draft documents too, and a line that needs its own CIDE or pump meter needs its own group.
  • The group is not shown on the invoice form, only on the fiscal document line.
