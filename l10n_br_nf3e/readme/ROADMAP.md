## Transmission

The NF3e is not transmitted yet: confirming the document generates, signs and
validates the XML, and sending to the authorizer (or cancelling through Odoo)
warns that the service is not available. The goal is a contribution to
`nfelib`, following the pattern of its CT-e and MDF-e client
(`nfelib/<doc>/client/`, built on `brazil_fiscal_client`):

- the SOAP bindings generated by xsdata from the WSDL of the services
  `NF3eRecepcaoSinc`, `NF3eRecepcaoEvento`, `NF3eConsulta` and
  `NF3eStatusServico`. `nfelib` 3.3.0 has the bindings for the NF3e layout and
  events, but not the WSDL or the SOAP bindings, and the WSDL are only served
  by the authorizers (`<service>?wsdl`);
- the server table of the five authorizers published on the NF3e Portal
  (`https://dfe-portal.svrs.rs.gov.br/Nf3e/Servicos`): MG, MS, MT, PR and
  SVRS, in production and homologation;
- the `NF3eClient` client with synchronous submission (XML compressed with
  gzip and encoded in base64), the event, the query and the service status.

With the client in `nfelib`, this module will send the XML stored in the
authorization event, handle the protocol (`protNF3e`, `nf3eProc`) and transmit
the cancellation event that `_nf3e_cancel_event_xml` already builds, signs and
validates (with the cancellation deadline rule of the MOC).

## Layout

- NT 2026.004 v1.01 (production on 2026-12-14): net item value (`vProdLiq`,
  `vItemLiq`), ICMS expected for advance payment, IBS and CBS in the judicial
  process group and the end of the normal NF3e with adjustment. `nfelib` 3.3.0
  ships the schema package of NT 2026.002 RTC v1.01: `l10n_br_nf3e_spec` must
  be regenerated when `nfelib` publishes the NT 2026.004 package.
- NT 2026.001 RTC (payment linking, `pgtoVinc` group and linking events).
- Offline contingency (`tpEmis` 2): `dhCont`, `xJust` and the QR Code `sign`
  parameter (MOC NF3e 1.00a, Overview, item 9.2).
- Electricity groups that the layout does not require and are not filled yet:
  tariffs by tariff period (`gTarif`, rules G111 and G112), tariff flag
  surcharge (`gAdBand`), item measurement (`gMedicao` and `gMedida`, rules
  G114 to G116; that is why the "contracted" quantity origin is rejected),
  contracted quantities (`gGrContrat`), energy compensation system (`gSCEE`),
  replacement (`gSub`), judicial process (`gJudic`, `gProcRef`), accounting
  accounts (`gContab`), items of a previous NF3e (`detItemAnt`, purposes 2 and
  3), advance payment, PIX, mailing address, parties authorized to access the
  XML (`autXML`) and technical manager (`gRespTec`).
- IBS and CBS: credit reversal (`gEstornoCred`), deferral, tax refund,
  municipal IBS (exported as zero today), government procurement
  (`gCompraGov`) and donation (`indDoacao`).

## Business rules

- Sign of each item in the total according to the nature of the
  classification code (`cClass`, MOC NF3e 1.00a, Overview, item 7.9) and the
  refund indicator: today all items are added.
- Validation of the allowed CFOP and of its compatibility with `cClass`
  (rules G108 and G109, items 7.8 and 7.9 of the MOC).
- Billing history (`gANEEL`) built from the previous NF3e of the same consumer
  unit; today it is entered in the document.
- Registration of the consumer unit and meters on the customer, with the
  classification code and the default unit of measure on the product.
- More than one `NFdet` group (up to 13) and more than 990 items.
- DANF3E (MOC NF3e, Annex II) and NF3e XML import.
