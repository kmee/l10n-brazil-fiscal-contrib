NF3e

Issuance of the Electronic Electricity Bill (NF3e, model 66), mandatory for electricity distributors and licensees (Ajuste SINIEF 01/19), from the l10n_br_fiscal fiscal document with document type 66.

The module injects the l10n_br_nf3e_spec models into the fiscal document and its lines (spec_driven_model stacking, following the same pattern as l10n_br_mdfe and l10n_br_cte) and:

  • numbers the document by series and builds the NF3e access key (MOC NF3e 1.00a, Overview, item 2.1: 7-digit numeric code, issuance type and authorizer site), with a modulo 11 check digit, including for alphanumeric CNPJ;
  • generates the XML with the groups ide, emit, dest, acessante (consumer unit), gMed (meters), NFdet (items with prod and imposto), total, gFat (invoice), gANEEL (billing history), infAdic and the infNF3eSupl QR Code (MOC NF3e 1.00a, Overview, item 9.2);
  • carries into the XML the taxes computed by the OCA fiscal engine: ICMS (group chosen by the CST), PIS, COFINS, withholdings and the item IBSCBS group with the IBSCBSTot total (NT 2025.001 RTC v1.14a);
  • computes vNF (MOC, rule G157) and vTotDFe, which repeats vNF in 2026 and adds IBS and CBS from 2027, by issue date (NT 2025.001 RTC v1.14a, item 9, rules 360 and 365, and NT 2026.004 v1.01);
  • signs the XML with the company certificate, validates it against the nfelib XSD and stores the signed XML in the authorization event of the document;
  • builds, signs and validates the XML of the cancellation event (110111).

Transmission to the authorizer is not available yet: see the roadmap.

Important

This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning. Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production. More details on development status

Configuration

On the company (Fiscal tab, NF3e):

  • choose the NF3e environment (homologation or production);
  • check the QR Code address. The default is the SVRS one (https://dfe-portal.svrs.rs.gov.br/nf3e/qrCode); MG, MT and SC publish their own addresses on the services page of the NF3e Portal (https://dfe-portal.svrs.rs.gov.br/Nf3e/Servicos), and SEFAZ rejects the QR Code with an address different from the expected one (MOC NF3e 1.00a, rule G164).

The company needs a CNPJ, state registration, state, city, digital certificate and the “Odoo Community” electronic document processor. Also create a series for document type 66.

Usage

  1. Create a fiscal document with document type 66, the customer, the fiscal operation and the NF3e series.
  2. In the NF3e tab, fill in the consumer unit (acessante), the invoice data (competence period, due date, next reading, payment line and automatic debit or bank and branch), the meters and the billing history (1 to 5 quantities, each with the 13 months that the layout requires).
  3. On each item, enter the electricity CFOP and, in the NF3e tab of the line, the item classification code, the unit of measure and the origin of the billed quantity. For IBS and CBS, enter the tax classification (cClassTrib): the fiscal engine computes the values.
  4. Confirm the document: the XML is generated, signed and validated, and is kept in the authorization event. Schema errors appear in the XML error message.

Known issues / Roadmap

Transmission

The NF3e is not transmitted yet: confirming the document generates, signs and validates the XML, and sending to the authorizer (or cancelling through Odoo) warns that the service is not available. The goal is a contribution to nfelib, following the pattern of its CT-e and MDF-e client (nfelib/<doc>/client/, built on brazil_fiscal_client):

  • the SOAP bindings generated by xsdata from the WSDL of the services NF3eRecepcaoSinc, NF3eRecepcaoEvento, NF3eConsulta and NF3eStatusServico. nfelib 3.3.0 has the bindings for the NF3e layout and events, but not the WSDL or the SOAP bindings, and the WSDL are only served by the authorizers (<service>?wsdl);
  • the server table of the five authorizers published on the NF3e Portal (https://dfe-portal.svrs.rs.gov.br/Nf3e/Servicos): MG, MS, MT, PR and SVRS, in production and homologation;
  • the NF3eClient client with synchronous submission (XML compressed with gzip and encoded in base64), the event, the query and the service status.

With the client in nfelib, this module will send the XML stored in the authorization event, handle the protocol (protNF3e, nf3eProc) and transmit the cancellation event that _nf3e_cancel_event_xml already builds, signs and validates (with the cancellation deadline rule of the MOC).

Layout

  • NT 2026.004 v1.01 (production on 2026-12-14): net item value (vProdLiq, vItemLiq), ICMS expected for advance payment, IBS and CBS in the judicial process group and the end of the normal NF3e with adjustment. nfelib 3.3.0 ships the schema package of NT 2026.002 RTC v1.01: l10n_br_nf3e_spec must be regenerated when nfelib publishes the NT 2026.004 package.
  • NT 2026.001 RTC (payment linking, pgtoVinc group and linking events).
  • Offline contingency (tpEmis 2): dhCont, xJust and the QR Code sign parameter (MOC NF3e 1.00a, Overview, item 9.2).
  • Electricity groups that the layout does not require and are not filled yet: tariffs by tariff period (gTarif, rules G111 and G112), tariff flag surcharge (gAdBand), item measurement (gMedicao and gMedida, rules G114 to G116; that is why the “contracted” quantity origin is rejected), contracted quantities (gGrContrat), energy compensation system (gSCEE), replacement (gSub), judicial process (gJudic, gProcRef), accounting accounts (gContab), items of a previous NF3e (detItemAnt, purposes 2 and 3), advance payment, PIX, mailing address, parties authorized to access the XML (autXML) and technical manager (gRespTec).
  • IBS and CBS: credit reversal (gEstornoCred), deferral, tax refund, municipal IBS (exported as zero today), government procurement (gCompraGov) and donation (indDoacao).

Business rules

  • Sign of each item in the total according to the nature of the classification code (cClass, MOC NF3e 1.00a, Overview, item 7.9) and the refund indicator: today all items are added.
  • Validation of the allowed CFOP and of its compatibility with cClass (rules G108 and G109, items 7.8 and 7.9 of the MOC).
  • Billing history (gANEEL) built from the previous NF3e of the same consumer unit; today it is entered in the document.
  • Registration of the consumer unit and meters on the customer, with the classification code and the default unit of measure on the product.
  • More than one NFdet group (up to 13) and more than 990 items.
  • DANF3E (MOC NF3e, Annex II) and NF3e XML import.
