BP-e

Issuance of the Electronic Passenger Ticket (BP-e, model 63, layout 1.00) from the fiscal document (l10n_br_fiscal.document) with document type 63 of l10n_br_fiscal, following the pattern of l10n_br_mdfe: the layout groups come from l10n_br_bpe_spec and are stacked onto the fiscal document.

The BP-e has been mandatory since 2019-07-01 for intercity, interstate and international passenger transport by road, water and rail (Ajuste SINIEF 01/17, clause 18-A, as worded by Ajuste SINIEF 22/18).

The module:

  • builds the BP-e XML: ide, emit (from the company), comp (from the document partner, when provided), infPassagem and infPassageiro, infViagem, infValorBPe (one Comp per fiscal document line), imp (line ICMS, the IBSCBS group and vTotDFe), pag, infAdic, infRespTec and the infBPeSupl QR Code;
  • computes IBS and CBS with the OCA fiscal engine (tax classification and line taxes) and fills the IBSCBS group of NT 2025.001 RTC;
  • applies the vTotDFe rule of NT 2026.004 v1.01: equal to vBP in 2026 and vBP plus IBS and CBS from 2027, by local issue date;
  • generates the access key, signs the XML with the company certificate and validates it against the BP-e XSD of nfelib;
  • transmits the BP-e (synchronous BPeRecepcao service, message compressed with GZip and encoded in Base64) and records the authorization protocol;
  • sends the cancellation (110111) and no-boarding (110115) events.

The transmission uses its own client (bpe_client), built on brazil_fiscal_client, following the same pattern as the CT-e and MDF-e clients of nfelib, which does not have a BP-e client yet. It follows MOC BP-e 1.00b (taxpayer guidance manual, items 3.3 and 3.4): SOAP 1.2, request in bpeDadosMsg, response in bpeResultMsg, no SOAP header. The authorizer addresses (MG, MS, MT, PR, SP and SVRS for the other states) and the QR Code address are those of the BP-e Portal (https://dfe-portal.svrs.rs.gov.br/BPe/Servicos).

Configuration

  • On the company, in the Fiscal tab, set the electronic document processor to “Odoo Community”, the A1 digital certificate and, in the BP-e tab, the environment (production or homologation).
  • Create a series for document type 63 (BP-e) on the company.
  • Configure the fiscal operation for ticket sales: the BP-e lines need a fiscal operation line so that the engine can compute ICMS, IBS and CBS.

Usage

  1. Create a fiscal document with document type 63 (BP-e).
  2. Each document line is a component of the ticket value (fare, toll, boarding fee…), chosen in the “BP-e Component Type” field of the line.
  3. In the BP-e tab, enter the origin and destination cities (or the location codes), the boarding and ticket validity dates, the passenger, the trip legs and the payment methods.
  4. Confirm the document to generate, sign and validate the XML, then send it for authorization.
  5. Once the BP-e is authorized, the “Cancel” and “No Boarding” buttons send the cancellation and no-boarding events.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • Move the BP-e web service client (bpe_client) to nfelib, in nfelib/bpe/client/v1_0, next to the CT-e and MDF-e clients, with the SOAP classes generated from the WSDL (the WSDL is only served with a client certificate, so the classes were declared by hand).
  • Check three homologation addresses on the BP-e Portal that seem swapped on the services page (MG: Consulta points to production and StatusServico to BPeConsulta; MS: Consulta points to MG). They were kept as published.
  • BP-e TM (metropolitan transport, BPeRecepcaoTM service) and BP-e TA (air transport, BPeRecepcaoTA, NT 2025.002): the models are in l10n_br_bpe_spec, but their issuance has its own structure (TM groups the trips by turnstile in detBPeTM) and was not implemented.
  • Offline contingency (tpEmis 2): QR Code with the sign parameter (MOC item 10.2), dhCont and xJust.
  • Replacement BP-e (tpBPe 3, infBPeSub) and the seat change and excess baggage events.
  • ICMSUFFim group (ICMS sharing with the destination state for final consumers), agencia group, autXML, ICMS CST 60 and deferral, reduction and monophasic treatment of IBS and CBS beyond gRed.
  • Payment linking (pgtoVinc, NT 2026.001) and the NT 2026.005 rules for Simples Nacional.
  • DABPE (auxiliary PDF document) and BP-e XML import.
